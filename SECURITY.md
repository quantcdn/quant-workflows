# Security Policy

QuantCDN takes the security of its software seriously. We welcome reports of
security vulnerabilities in this project from researchers, customers and the
public.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.**

Report a vulnerability in this repository privately using GitHub's
**[Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/quantcdn/quant-workflows/security/advisories/new)**
("Report a vulnerability" under the **Security** tab).

For vulnerabilities in the QuantCDN platform or services (rather than this
source code), email **security@quantcdn.io**.

## Before you start

Please read our **[Vulnerability Disclosure Statement](https://www.quantcdn.io/legal/vulnerability-disclosure)**, which
sets out:

- what is in and out of scope,
- the rules of engagement we ask you to follow,
- the **safe harbour** we offer for good-faith research, and
- what you can expect from us, including coordinated disclosure.

By testing in good faith and in accordance with that statement, your research is
authorised under our safe harbour.

## What to expect

We will acknowledge your report, validate and triage it (scoring severity with
CVSS), keep you informed, remediate confirmed issues within our published
timeframes, and credit you in the resulting advisory if you would like us to.
This is a vulnerability disclosure program, not a bug bounty — we do not offer
monetary rewards.
